Crash types

Campaigns built around how the crash happened

People search and respond differently after different crashes. Each crash type gets its own ads, landing page and questions, so every message speaks to one situation.

Rear-end collisions

The most common crash type, with ads aimed at drivers dealing with neck, back and soft-tissue injuries.

Intersection & T-bone crashes

Messaging for side-impact crashes at lights, stop signs and left turns.

Uninsured & underinsured drivers

Campaigns for people hit by a driver with no insurance or not enough coverage.

Rideshare accidents

Passengers and drivers hurt during Uber or Lyft trips, where insurance questions are complex.

Commercial & delivery vehicles

Crashes involving delivery vans, company cars and trucks, routed to the right case team.

Pedestrians & cyclists

People struck by a car while walking or riding, often with serious injuries.

Built-in qualification

Only real car accident cases reach intake

A short form screens every inquiry before your team sees it. People who were at fault, weren't hurt or already have a lawyer are filtered out, so intake spends its time on cases it can sign.

Example qualifying form

  1. 1When did the accident happen?
  2. 2Were you or a passenger injured?
  3. 3Did you get medical treatment?
  4. 4Was the other driver at fault?
  5. 5Do you already have a lawyer?

Questions are set with your firm before launch.
