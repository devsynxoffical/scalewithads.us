How Much Do MVA Leads Cost? Pricing Models and Real Campaign Numbers

What MVA leads cost under each pricing model, what drives the price, our published Meta Ads cost per lead, and how to work out your real cost per signed case.

MVA leads are inquiries from people injured in a motor vehicle accident who want to speak to a lawyer. What you pay for them depends less on a single market price and more on how they are generated, who else receives them and how well they are qualified.

The four ways firms pay for MVA leads

  1. 1Shared pay-per-lead: you pay per lead, and the same person may be sold to several firms.
  2. 2Exclusive pay-per-lead: the vendor sells each lead to one firm only, usually at a higher price.
  3. 3Pay per signed case or retainer: you only pay when the client signs, at a much higher price per case.
  4. 4Generating your own: you run ads in your own account, pay the platform directly and own every lead and the data.

What drives the price of an MVA lead

  • State and city: competitive legal markets cost more to advertise in.
  • Case type: car, truck, motorcycle and rideshare cases are priced differently.
  • Exclusivity: a lead no other firm is calling is worth more than a shared one.
  • Qualification: leads screened for injury, fault and representation cost more but sign more often.
  • Speed of follow-up: a lead called within minutes is worth more than one called the next day.

Past results, not a quote

These are lead costs from specific campaigns in specific markets. Your cost will depend on your state, case types, offer and how fast your intake team follows up.

Why cost per signed case matters more than cost per lead

A cheap lead that never signs is expensive. Divide your total lead spend by the cases you actually sign. As an example, 50 leads at $100 each with a 7% sign rate produce 3.5 cases, about $1,429 per signed case. If exclusive leads called instantly signed at 12% instead, the same spend would cost about $833 per case. You can try your own numbers with the calculator on our exclusive MVA leads page.

Questions to ask before you buy MVA leads

  • Is each lead sold to my firm only, or to several firms?
  • How is each lead verified, and what questions does the form ask?
  • How fast is the lead delivered, and in what format?
  • Do I own the data and the ads if I stop?
  • Can you show results with dates and dashboards, not just testimonials?

Key takeaways

  • MVA lead prices depend on the model, market, case type, exclusivity and qualification.
  • Our published Meta Ads campaigns ran at $17.13 to $26.93 per lead.
  • Judge every source on cost per signed case, not price per lead.
  • Exclusive, qualified leads that your team calls within minutes usually sign at a higher rate. For car crash campaigns specifically, see car accident leads.

Frequently asked questions

How much do MVA leads cost?

It depends on the pricing model, state, case type and how exclusive and verified the lead is. In our own published Meta Ads campaigns, MVA and personal injury leads cost $17.13 to $26.93 each. The number that matters more is your cost per signed case.

Are cheaper MVA leads worse?

Not always, but very cheap leads are often shared with several firms or loosely qualified, which lowers your sign rate. Compare offers on cost per signed case, not price per lead.

How do I calculate cost per signed case?

Divide what you spend on leads by the number of cases you sign. For example, 50 leads at $100 each that produce 3.5 signed cases cost about $1,429 per case. These are example numbers; use your own.

Is it cheaper to generate MVA leads yourself?

Running ads in your own account means you pay the ad platform directly and own the data, but it takes setup, creative, qualification and fast follow-up to work. Many firms use an agency to build and run it.
