In short

What is personal injury marketing?

Personal injury marketing is how a law firm attracts people who were recently injured and turns them into signed cases. It combines advertising, screening, fast intake follow-up and tracking from first contact to signed retainer. The best personal injury law firm marketing is judged on cost per signed case, not on clicks or impressions.

The system

The 4 parts of personal injury marketing

Most firms that are unhappy with their marketing are not short of traffic. They lose cases between the click and the signature. A personal injury marketing system has four parts, and a weak link in any one of them raises your cost per signed case.

Advertising

Ads that reach people shortly after an accident or injury, with one message per case type and creatives your firm approves.

Screening

Short forms that ask about the incident date, injuries, treatment, fault and representation before anyone at your firm picks up the phone.

Intake speed

An instant text and email to every new inquiry, and a call from your intake team within minutes, before another firm reaches them.

Reporting

Every inquiry tracked from first contact to signed case, so budget moves toward the ads and audiences that produce cases.

How we run personal injury marketing

As a personal injury marketing agency, our job is to make your intake team busier with the right people. Here is the process, from the first call to weekly optimization.

  1. 01

    Strategy call

    We map your case types, target areas, intake capacity and what a signed case is worth to your firm.

  2. 02

    Audience research

    We find the audiences and interests that reach injured people in your market, not only the obvious legal interests.

  3. 03

    Ads and landing pages

  4. 04

  6. 06

    Optimize on signed cases

    Each week, budget moves toward the ads and audiences that produce signed cases, not just form fills.
