In short

What makes a good personal injury lead generation company?

A good personal injury lead generation company sends each lead to your firm only, screens leads before delivery, verifies contact details, delivers in real time, is clear about who owns the data, follows state bar advertising rules and publishes dated results. Compare providers on cost per signed case, not on price per lead alone.

The criteria

8 criteria for choosing a PI lead generation company

Score every provider, including us, on the same 8 points. The full checklist is in our guide on how to choose a legal lead generation company.

1. Exclusivity

Is each lead sent to your firm only, or to several firms at once?

2. Screening

Does every lead answer questions on the incident, injuries, treatment and representation?

3. Verification

Are phone numbers verified before delivery?

4. Speed

Are leads delivered in real time, with automatic follow-up?

5. Ownership

Who keeps the ads, pages and data if you stop?

6. Compliance

Are ads reviewed against your state bar's advertising rules?

7. Reporting

Can you see cost per lead and cost per signed case by case type?

8. Proof

Are results published with dates and dashboards, not only testimonials?

Honest trade-offs

When another model may suit you better

Running campaigns in your own account is not right for every firm. A different provider type may be a better fit if:

  • You need leads this week and cannot wait 14 to 21 days for a build
  • Your firm has no one available to call leads within minutes
  • You only want to pay when a case signs, whatever the price per case
  • You want a small test before committing to any ad budget
  • You prefer not to approve ads and landing pages yourself
  • You only take a handful of cases a month and do not need a system
Red flags

Red flags when buying personal injury leads

These warning signs apply to every type of provider. For pricing, see how much MVA leads cost.

  1. 01

    Vague exclusivity

    The provider will not say in writing whether leads are shared.

  2. 02

    No screening

    Forms ask only for a name and phone number.

  3. 03

    Undated results

    Big numbers with no dates, dashboards or context.

  4. 04

    No data ownership

    You lose the source, history and data the day you stop.

  5. 05

    Outcome promises

    Ads or sales pitches that guarantee case results or settlements.

  6. 06

    No compliance review

    Nobody checks ads against your state bar's rules.
