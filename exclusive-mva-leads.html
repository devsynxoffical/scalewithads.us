What are MVA leads?

Three ways to get MVA leads

Buy shared leads: fast but sold to several firms. Buy exclusive leads from a vendor: better, but you do not own the source. Or run your own campaigns: exclusive leads and a system your firm owns. Focused on car crashes? See car accident leads.

Typical lead vendor

Shared MVA leads

  • Fast to start
  • Pay per lead
  • Often sold to several firms
  • You race other firms to call first
  • No control over ads or targeting

Premium lead vendor

Exclusive vendor leads

  • Not shared with other firms
  • Pay per lead or per retainer
  • Higher price per lead
  • Vendor owns the ads and data
  • Volume stops when you stop paying
