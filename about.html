What is Scale With Ads?

Scale With Ads is a Meta ads agency that runs Facebook and Instagram campaigns for law firms and service businesses, including exclusive personal injury, MVA, mass tort and workers comp leads. Campaigns run in the client's own ad account, every lead is screened, and published results include 530 leads in 7 days for a Texas personal injury firm.

Key facts

What we are
A Meta ads agency that runs Facebook and Instagram campaigns, with screening and intake follow-up built in.
Who we serve
Personal injury and other law firms in the United States, plus agencies that advertise for attorneys.
Case types
Personal injury, motor vehicle accidents (car, truck, motorcycle, rideshare), workers compensation and mass tort.
Pricing model
Firms pay Meta directly for ad spend in their own ad account, plus a management fee scoped on the strategy call.
Setup time
Campaigns typically go live 14 to 21 days after the kickoff call.
Published results
76 MVA leads in 5 days at $17.13 (Jul 2026), 203 MVA leads in 21 days at $18.69 (Jul 2026), 530 leads in 7 days at $26.93 for a Texas PI firm (Jun 2026).
Leadership
Founded and led by Vaishali Kapoor (Founder) and Gaurav Kapoor (Co-Founder).
Compliance
Every ad and landing page is approved by the client firm against its state bar's advertising rules. Ads never promise case outcomes.
