How much do personal injury leads cost?

There is no single market price. What you pay depends on the pricing model, your state, the case type and whether the lead is exclusive and screened. In our own published Meta ads campaigns, personal injury and MVA leads cost $17.13 to $26.93 each. The number that matters more is your cost per signed case.

What drives the price of a personal injury lead

  • Market: competitive states and cities cost more to advertise in.
  • Case type: truck, motorcycle, workplace and product cases are priced differently from car accidents.
  • Exclusivity: a lead no other firm is calling is worth more.
  • Screening: leads checked for injury, treatment, fault and representation cost more but sign more often.
  • Delivery speed: a lead delivered in real time is worth more than one delivered tomorrow.

How to calculate cost per signed case

  1. 1Add up everything you spent on one lead source in a period, including fees.
  2. 2Count the cases you signed from that source in the same period.
  3. 3Divide spend by signed cases.
  4. 4Repeat for each source and compare them side by side.

As an example with made-up numbers: if you spend $5,000 on a source and sign 4 cases from it, your cost per signed case is $1,250. A cheaper source that signs only 1 case for $3,000 costs $3,000 per signed case. Use your own numbers.

How to lower your cost per signed case

  • Use exclusive leads, so your team is not racing other firms.
  • Ask screening questions before the call.
  • Respond within minutes with an instant text and a call. See legal intake.
  • Run one campaign per case type, so every lead matches what your firm takes.

Frequently asked questions

How much do personal injury leads cost?

It depends on the pricing model, state, case type and how exclusive and screened the lead is. In our own published Meta ads campaigns, personal injury and MVA leads cost $17.13 to $26.93 each. Cost per signed case is the number to compare.

Why do personal injury lead prices vary so much?

Prices change with the state and city, the case type, whether the lead is shared or exclusive, how well it is screened and how it is delivered. A cheap shared lead and an exclusive screened lead are different products.

Is it cheaper to pay per signed case?

You pay nothing for leads that do not sign, but the price per signed case is usually much higher than paying per lead or running your own ads. It suits firms that want no upfront risk.

How do I calculate my cost per signed case?

Add up everything you spend on a lead source in a period and divide it by the number of cases you signed from that source in the same period.

What lowers the cost of personal injury leads?

Exclusive leads, good screening questions, fast follow-up and one campaign per case type. These raise your sign rate, which lowers your cost per signed case even if the price per lead stays the same.
