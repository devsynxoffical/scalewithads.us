How it works

From Facebook Ad to Signed Case

01

Step 01

Sniper Method audience research

We map hidden Facebook audiences around each case type instead of bidding on the same legal and insurance interests every other firm targets.

02

Step 02

Ads and landing pages

Video and image ads plus case-specific landing pages that speak to one accident type at a time, reviewed with your firm before anything goes live.

03

Step 03

Built-in qualification

Short qualifying questions, such as when the accident happened, whether there were injuries and whether the person already has a lawyer, filter out inquiries your intake team can't use.

04

Step 04

Instant follow-up

New inquiries get an immediate SMS and email response and land in your CRM, so speed-to-lead doesn't depend on someone watching the inbox.

05

Step 05

Optimize on signed cases

We feed qualified and signed-case outcomes back into the campaigns, so budget moves toward the audiences and ads that produce real cases.
